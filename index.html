Brazilian Root®

Developed by a solo developer, Brazilian Root® is a fast-paced, hardcore first-person shooter set against the backdrop of fictionalized Rio de Janeiro environments.

Storyline

Players assume the role of Benjamin, a former special operations soldier who retired from military duty to enter politics and drive social change. Just 30 days before the governor election, his daughter is mysteriously kidnapped. Forced to rely on his former tactical skills, Benjamin embarks on a relentless mission to rescue his daughter and seek justice.

Key Features

  • Skill-Focused Gameplay: Pure shooter action free of Quick Time Events (QTEs).
  • High-Stakes Challenge: Single-checkpoint per level design that demands tactical movement and precision.
  • Visuals & Audio: Built on CryEngine, featuring dynamic audio design powered by Audiokinetic Wwise©.
