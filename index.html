WELCOME TO ROULETTEGEDDON!

Welcome to Roulettegeddon - where the wheel keeps spinning, the dead keep talking, and your financial decisions somehow keep getting worse.

At its rotten heart, Roulettegeddon is a fully playable traditional roulette game wrapped in a dark, grotesque world filled with zombies, black humor, strange contraptions and questionable ways of making money.

Place classic roulette bets, chase your lucky numbers and try to grow your balance while sharing the table with Rotten Bob - a sarcastic zombie who has somehow managed to lose almost everything... including his body.

ONE DEVELOPER - 2026
A note from the developer

Patria Games is now a solo studio.

Patria Games began as a two-person company, but as of 2026 it is run by one solo developer. Development on Brazilian Root started back in 2014, and the years since then brought both milestones and setbacks: family changes, a hacked website, personal loss and, after the final patch, a hard drive failure that destroyed the core backup files for the game.

Because of that backup loss, Brazilian Root can no longer receive updates. Please keep that in mind before buying it and when writing a Steam review. Older review replies were written by the former co-founder; today, every response is written personally by the current developer. Right now, all ongoing development is focused on Roulettegeddon.

Thank you for all the support, patience and love shown to Patria Games.

Best regards, Leo

The studio

Independent
by design.

Founded in June 2015 in Rio de Janeiro, Patria Games was created to turn independent game development into something tangible even with a small team and limited resources.

Its first major project, Brazilian Root, required four years of development and was released on Steam in 2018. Today, the studio remains lean and focused on the original development of Roulettegeddon.
