The studio

Independent
by design.

Founded in June 2015 in Rio de Janeiro, Patria Games was created to turn independent game development into something tangible — even with a small team and limited resources.

Its first major project, Brazilian Root®, required four years of development and was released on Steam in 2018. Today, the studio remains lean and focused on the original development of Roulettegeddon.
